# Blanca Condo Cluster Contribution Agreement  

The Blanca compute cluster is a service provided by Research Computing within OIT at the University of Colorado Boulder (referred to as “CURC”). Blanca aggregates compute nodes contributed by individual researchers (referred to as “Contributors”) into a heterogeneous compute cluster managed by CURC.


**The parties agree to the following under this agreement:**

<ol>
  <li> Terms of Contribution
    <ol type="a">
      <li> Ownership: Contributions remain the property of the Contributor and may be fitted with the Contributor’s asset tag. Contributors are responsible for facilitating their own capital asset inventory process required by their institution. OIT will assist with access but requires 72 hours advanced notice for in-person inventory counts.</li>
      <li>Removal Rights: Contributors may remove their contributions from Blanca if the environment no longer meets their needs. Removal also terminates access to Blanca-related benefits.</li>
      <li>Administrative Access: Administrative (e.g., “root”) access to the Blanca environment, including contributed resources, is reserved for Research Computing operational staff and its delegates.</li>
      <li>Installation Location: Contributions are installed in a CU data center at the discretion of Research Computing.</li>
      <li>Operating Environment: Contributed nodes are provisioned with a Linux-based OS and environment at the discretion of Research Computing.</li>
      <li>Core Services Access: Nodes are provisioned with access to Research Computing Core services and the PetaLibrary, to the extent technically feasible.</li>
      <li>Acceptance Criteria: Research Computing reserves the right to determine which contributions are accepted into Blanca. Recommended configurations are published on the <a href="https://www.colorado.edu/rc/resources/blanca">Research Computing website</a>.</li>
      <li>Cluster Configuration: Contributed nodes are generally configured as part of a Slurm batch compute cluster. Alternative configurations or access methods may be supported at CURC's discretion to accommodate specialized research workloads. CURC retains authority to determine and approve configurations based on technical feasibility, operational requirements, security, and the needs of the shared Blanca environment.</li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li>Usage Model
    <ol type="a">
      <li>Preemptable Access: All Blanca compute nodes accept preemptable jobs from all Blanca contributors and their appointees. These jobs may be preempted when Contributors or their appointees schedule jobs to their own contributed nodes. </li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li>Hardware Lifecycle, Facility Costs, and Custodianship
    <ol type="a">
      <li>Contributors are responsible for the upfront cost of their contributed node(s), warranties, and the cost of cables and related cabling required to connect their contributed hardware to the Blanca environment. The estimated cost of required cables will be quoted at the same time as the hardware.</li>
      <li>Research Computing covers the ongoing costs of operating the nodes as part of the Blanca environment during the warranty period, including data center space, power, cooling, and routing administration.</li>
      <li>Right of Refusal: For equipment purchased directly by contributing departments, not through Research Computing, Contributors agree to give Research Computing right of first refusal before surplusing or otherwise disposing of any Blanca contributions. For equipment purchased by Research Computing/OIT using contributor funding, standard OIT property disposal procedures apply. All contributed hardware will be deployed under Research Computing’s custodianship as Property Manager and operated as part of the aggregate Blanca environment for use by all active Blanca contributors and their appointees.</li>
      <li>Unrestricted Removal: This agreement does not restrict the Contributor’s right to remove their contributions for re-use outside of Blanca at no cost to the Contributor, subject to applicable property transfer procedures. This action would effectively terminate the MOU on the date of removal per Section 5 of this agreement.</li>
      <li>Warranty and Service Life: All nodes must include, at minimum, a five-year warranty and are guaranteed to remain in service for that period. Unless you choose not to, CURC will continue to operate your nodes for an additional two years beyond the initial 5-year warranty expiration.
        <ol type="i">
          <li> i. You have the option to purchase a 2-year extended warranty for years 6-7, which will cover hardware component failures (e.g., RAM, SSDs, CPUs, fans, etc.) if they occur. If you do not purchase an extended warranty, we will continue to operate your nodes during years 6-7 provided the nodes continue to meet CU data center security requirements and do not experience hardware component failures (e.g., RAM, SSDs, CPUs, fans, etc.). Nodes not under warranty will be decommissioned if hardware components fail during this period.</li>
          <li>CURC will contact Contributors near the end of year seven to coordinate decommissioning. Nodes must be retired and surplussed after seven years.</li>
        </ol>
      </li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li>User Policies
    <ol type="a">
      <li>Contributors and any individuals they authorize to access their Blanca hardware are subject CURC’s User Policies, as published and updated at: <a href="https://curc.readthedocs.io/en/latest/additional-resources/policies.html">https://curc.readthedocs.io/en/latest/additional-resources/policies.html</a></li>
    </ol>
  </li>
  <li>Termination
    <ol type="a">
      <li>This agreement will automatically terminate on whichever comes sooner: 1) two years beyond the initial 5-year warranty expiration or, if the contributor purchases the optional 2-year extended warranty, when the 7-year warranty expires or 2) when all equipment is removed from the data center or when ownership of the equipment is transferred to OI </li>
    </ol>
  </li>
</ol>
